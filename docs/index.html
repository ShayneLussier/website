About

I'm a developer working across software engineering and business intelligence. Over the past five years, I've built data pipelines, data warehouses, and analytics solutions, first at L3HARRIS and now at JAMP Pharma Group.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me out on my motorcycle, staying active, or cheering on the Habs, whatever the standings say.
